Ideally, treatment is two weeks apart. #### ADD MORE ###

Only treatments that were less than 28 days apart were considered. For example, if an animal was treated on 5/29/2025 and 8/5/2025 with sample collection on 9/3/2025, they were considered to be treated only once because the first treatment was over 28 days apart from the second treatment.

Additionally, if treatment occurred less than two weeks before collection date, the animal would still be shedding toxocara and would therefore likely test positive. To adjust for this, treatments administered less than fourteen days prior to collection date were excluded from the treatment count.

As an example, the following shows one case with 32 days between Nemex doses. This case was considered to only receive one Nemex treatment, not two.
